What it is

A rhythm game where every note is a stroke

Row Hero is Guitar Hero on the water. Notes stream down towards your boat, and every one you hit in time pulls your crew through the water. Hit a streak and the boat flies. Miss and the crew beside you starts to move away.

It was made for an older brother who rows and won at Henley Royal Regatta, so the races, venues and crews come from real rowing.

Career mode

Race a full season, venue by venue. Each regatta is a run of races with a boss race at the end.

Pick your crew

Between races, spend your winnings in the shop on new rowers. Each one brings a perk that changes how your boat races.

Free play

Replay any song you've unlocked, at any venue, with the crew you've built. Add your own songs too.

Leaderboards & achievements

Post your best scores online, chase achievements, and look out for the secret bosses.

Getting started

Install in a minute

  1. Download the installerClick the yellow button to get RowHeroInstaller.exe from GitHub.
  2. Run itOpen the file from your Downloads folder and follow the installer.
  3. Launch and rowOpen Row Hero from the launcher. It checks for updates and shows what's new each time you start it, so you won't need to download the installer again.

Windows warning? The installer isn't signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may say it "protected your PC". Click More info, then Run anyway.
