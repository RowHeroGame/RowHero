Row Hero is a free rowing rhythm game for Windows.

Features

  • Career mode: Race a full season, with all four iconic venues of schoolboy rowing.
  • Free play: Play any song you want at any venue, or add your own songs.
  • Leaderboards: Log in with Google to post your best scores and see how you stack up.
  • Play offline: No account needed. Your saves stay on your own computer.
