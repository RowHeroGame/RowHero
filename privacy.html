Privacy policy

Last updated 5 November 2026

Short version: Row Hero works fully offline. If you choose to log in with Google, we store your display name, your profile picture and your best scores so they can appear on the online leaderboards. We don't sell your data, show ads, or use it for anything else.

Who we are

Row Hero is a free rhythm game for Windows made by an independent developer in the United Kingdom ("we", "us"). We are responsible for the personal data described here. You can contact us at lfairowl12@gmail.com.

If you don't log in

You can play the whole game without an account. Your save files and settings stay on your own computer and are never sent to us.

What we collect when you log in with Google

Logging in is optional and is only used for leaderboards. When you press "Log in with Google", Google asks you to share basic profile information with Row Hero. We only ask for the standard openid, email and profile permissions.

DataWhere it comes fromWhy we use it
Your Google account ID and email addressGoogle sign-inTo create and identify your Row Hero account. Your email is held by our sign-in provider (Firebase Authentication) and is never shown to other players.
Your display nameGoogle, or a name you set in the gameShown next to your scores on the leaderboards.
Your profile pictureA link to your Google photo, or a small picture you choose in the gameShown on your account button and on the leaderboards.
Your best scoresThe game, when you win a raceBest score and accuracy for each song, best points for each regatta, and when they were set. Shown on the leaderboards.

We do not collect your contacts, location, payment details, or any other Google data. We do not use analytics or advertising tools.

Who can see it

Other players who are logged in can see your display name, profile picture and scores on the leaderboards. Nobody else can see your email address.

Where it is stored

  • On Google's servers: account and leaderboard data is stored using Google Firebase (Authentication and Realtime Database). Google processes it on our behalf under its own terms and security measures.
  • On your computer: to keep you logged in, the game saves a sign-in token in the Windows registry with the game's other settings. Pressing Log out in account settings removes it.
  • GitHub: the installer and game updates are downloaded from GitHub. When the launcher checks for updates, GitHub receives your IP address like any website would. We don't receive it.

Use of Google user data

Row Hero's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We only use Google user data to sign you in and show your name and picture on the leaderboards. We don't sell it, transfer it to anyone else, or use it for advertising.

How long we keep it

We keep your account and scores until you ask us to delete them, or until we shut down the online features. We may remove test scores, inactive accounts, or scores that break the terms of service.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask to see, correct or delete your data, or object to how we use it. To delete your account and all of your scores, email lfairowl12@gmail.com from the Google account you logged in with, and we'll do it within 30 days. You can also remove Row Hero's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

If you're unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Our legal basis for using your data is your consent when you choose to log in, and our legitimate interest in running fair leaderboards.

Children

Row Hero is not directed at children under 13, and online features require a Google account, which has its own age rules. If you believe a child has given us data without permission, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page. If a change affects how we use your data, we'll mention it in the launcher's changelog.
